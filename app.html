DigitCheck

Paste an IBAN, an ISBN or a Luhn number. It tells you if the check digits add up, and where it goes wrong if they do not.

Make the check digits

What this proves: the check digits are consistent, so a typo is very likely caught. It does not prove that an account exists, that a book was ever printed, or that a card number is real. IBAN: country length and character format from the table on the Wikipedia IBAN page (111 countries, transcribed at the time of writing; some countries use national check digits inside the account part that are not checked here), then the ISO 13616 mod 97 test (move the first four characters to the end, letters become 10 to 35, remainder must be 1). ISBN-10: digits weighted 10 down to 1, sum divisible by 11, X means 10. ISBN-13: weights alternate 1 and 3, sum divisible by 10. Luhn (ISO/IEC 7812-1): double every second digit from the right of the payload, subtract 9 from results above 9, sum divisible by 10. Do not paste real card numbers or account numbers into any page you do not trust; this one runs in your browser and stores nothing.
