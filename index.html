DigitCheck
One wrong digit and the payment bounces. Catch it first.
Open the app
GB82 WEST 1234 5698 7654 32 is a valid IBAN. Change the last digit and it fails: remainder mod 97 is no longer 1. 0-306-40615-2 converts to 978-0-306-40615-7.

What it does

  • IBAN: country length, format and the mod 97 check, with the reason when it fails
  • ISBN-10 and ISBN-13 with conversion between them
  • Luhn check, and the digit a payload needs
  • Make the IBAN check digits from an account part
Checks digits only, not whether an account or book exists. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
